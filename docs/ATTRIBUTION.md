## Development-branch notices

GitHub's Compare & pull request notices announce recent pushes to development
branches. They are not build errors or proof of missing app changes. Reviewed
release snapshots use separate provenance records. Do not merge an experimental
updater branch merely to remove a notice: its keys, feed and installer remain
unconfigured. Historical branches and tags remain available for audit.
